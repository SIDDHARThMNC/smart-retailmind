Return and Refund Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of the purchase date for a full refund, provided the item is in its original condition and packaging. Items that have been used, damaged, or altered are not eligible for return.

To initiate a return, the customer must present the original receipt or order confirmation. Returns without proof of purchase will not be accepted.

Refunds are processed within 7 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Cash refunds are available only for cash purchases made at physical store locations.

Electronics and appliances must be returned within 15 days of purchase. The product must be in its original sealed packaging. If the seal is broken, the item can only be exchanged for the same product or a store credit.

Grocery and perishable items are not eligible for return unless the product is found to be defective or expired at the time of purchase. In such cases, the customer should contact the store within 24 hours of purchase.

Fashion and clothing items can be returned within 30 days if they are unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached. Items purchased during clearance sales are final sale and cannot be returned.

Beauty and personal care products can be returned only if they are unopened and in their original packaging. For hygiene reasons, opened beauty products are not eligible for return.

Home and furniture items can be returned within 30 days. Large furniture items require a scheduled pickup. A restocking fee of 10 percent may apply for furniture returns.

If a product is found to be defective within the warranty period, the customer is entitled to a free repair, replacement, or full refund depending on the nature of the defect and the product category.

For online orders, the return shipping cost is borne by the customer unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect item. In such cases, the company will arrange a free return pickup.

Exchanges are allowed for the same product in a different size or color, subject to availability. If the desired item is not available, a store credit will be issued.

Store credits are valid for 12 months from the date of issue and can be used for any purchase across all store locations and the online platform.

For any return or refund queries, customers can contact the customer support team or visit the nearest store.
