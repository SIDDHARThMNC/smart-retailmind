Discount and Promotions Policy

RetailMind offers discounts and promotional offers throughout the year to provide value to its customers. All discounts are subject to the terms and conditions mentioned at the time of the offer.

Regular discounts are applied automatically at the time of billing. Customers do not need to request a discount separately if it is already listed on the product tag or the website.

Seasonal sale events are held four times a year, typically in January, April, July, and October. During these events, discounts of up to 40 percent are offered on selected products across all categories.

Festive discounts are offered during major festivals and holidays. These discounts are time-limited and apply only to specific product categories as announced in the promotional communication.

Loyalty program members receive an additional 5 percent discount on all purchases. Loyalty points are accumulated at the rate of 1 point per 100 rupees spent and can be redeemed for future purchases.

Bulk purchase discounts are available for orders above 10 units of the same product. A discount of 5 to 15 percent is applied based on the quantity ordered. Bulk discounts are not applicable on already discounted items.

Coupon codes can be applied at the time of checkout for online orders. Each coupon code can be used only once per customer account. Coupon codes cannot be combined with other ongoing offers unless explicitly stated.

Employee discounts are available to all full-time and part-time employees of RetailMind. Employees receive a flat 15 percent discount on all purchases. This discount is not transferable and requires employee ID verification at the time of purchase.

Discounts are not applicable on gift cards, service charges, delivery fees, or extended warranty plans.

The company reserves the right to modify, suspend, or cancel any discount or promotional offer at any time without prior notice. In case of any dispute regarding a discount, the decision of the store manager will be final.

Price matching is available for identical products sold by authorized retailers at a lower price. Customers must provide proof of the lower price at the time of purchase. Price matching is not available for online-only deals or clearance sales.

For discount-related queries, customers can contact the promotions team at the store or through the website.
