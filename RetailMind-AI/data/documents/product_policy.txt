Product Quality and Warranty Policy

All products sold at RetailMind stores are sourced from verified and authorized suppliers. The company ensures that every product meets the applicable quality standards before being made available for sale.

Electronics products come with a standard manufacturer warranty of 1 year from the date of purchase. Extended warranty plans are available for purchase at the time of billing. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal usage conditions. Physical damage, water damage, and damage caused by unauthorized repairs are not covered under warranty.

Fashion and clothing items do not carry a standard warranty. However, if a product is found to have a manufacturing defect such as stitching issues or fabric tears within 15 days of purchase, the customer is eligible for a free replacement.

Grocery products are sold with clearly marked expiry dates. The company ensures that no product with less than 30 percent of its shelf life remaining is placed on the shelves. If a customer receives an expired product, they should report it immediately for a full refund.

Home and furniture products carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. This includes issues such as broken joints, faulty mechanisms, and material defects. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

Beauty and personal care products are sourced from licensed manufacturers and comply with applicable safety regulations. Customers with known allergies are advised to check the ingredient list before purchase. The company is not liable for allergic reactions caused by individual sensitivities.

All products are subject to availability. The company reserves the right to discontinue any product without prior notice. In case a product ordered online is out of stock, the customer will be notified and offered a full refund or an alternative product.

Counterfeit or unauthorized products are strictly not sold. If a customer suspects they have received a counterfeit product, they should report it to the store manager or contact the support team immediately.

Product images on the website and in promotional materials are for illustrative purposes only. Actual product appearance may vary slightly due to photography and display settings.

For product-related queries, customers can reach the product support team through the website or at the nearest store.
